Smart electric bikes add GPS tracking, 4G cellular security, motor customization tools, and ride data tracking on top of standard electric assist. On the Aventon Current ADV and Current EXP , these features are bundled into the ACU (Aventon Control Unit), giving you real-time location visibility, anti-theft protection, and control over how the motor feels, all from the Aventon app . This article breaks down every ACU feature by category so you know exactly what you're getting.
