"Worth It" Is a Math Question, Not a Feelings Question Whether an e-bike is worth it comes down to two numbers: what it costs to buy and run, and what it replaces. For someone who rides it instead of driving, the upfront price gets offset over time by everything they're not spending on gas, parking, and car maintenance. For someone who'd otherwise leave it in the garage, the same price is just a price.
