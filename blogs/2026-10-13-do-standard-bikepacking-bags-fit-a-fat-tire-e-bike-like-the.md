Why Fat-Tire Geometry Changes the Bag-Fit Question Most bikepacking bag guides are written with a traditional touring or gravel bike in mind — a slim aluminum or steel frame, an external battery-free front triangle, and standard rack mounting points that have looked roughly the same for decades. A fat-tire e-bike like the Aventon Aventure 3 or Aventure M shares almost none of that geometry.
