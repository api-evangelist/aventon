Why This Decision Comes Down to Range, Not Features If you're choosing between the Aventon Aventure 3 and Aventure M specifically for bikepacking, most of the feature list is a wash — both run 26x4.0" fat tires, both carry a 400-lb total payload, both take a rear rack. The decision that actually matters is range once you're loaded and multiple days from a charger, because shorter winter daylight leaves less margin to recover from a battery miscalculation mid-route than a summer trip would.
