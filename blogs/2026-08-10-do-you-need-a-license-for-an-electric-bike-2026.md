If you're asking do you need a license to ride an electric bike, you're not alone — and you're not wrong to be confused. One source says yes, another says no, and somehow New Jersey keeps coming up without anyone explaining why. Here's the truth: the answer depends on two things — what type of e-bike you're buying and which state you live in.
