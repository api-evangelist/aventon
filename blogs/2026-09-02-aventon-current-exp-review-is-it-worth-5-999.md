You've narrowed the Aventon Current line down to the EXP. Not the ADV — the EXP specifically, with the carbon front triangle, the wireless SRAM shifting, and the premium suspension tune. At $5,999, that's a real $2,000 premium over the ADV's $3,999, and before you commit to it, you want to know something no spec sheet will tell you: does the upgrade actually change how the bike rides, or are you paying $2,000 for a lighter frame and a badge that says "carbon"?
